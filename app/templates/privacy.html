Privacy Policy

This is a personal CDN run by Amit Dutta. The short version: everything you upload is public, and I keep a few technical details about uploads so I can stop abuse. Last updated: November 2026.

Your files are public

Anything you upload is public to the whole world, and anyone with the link can open it. Do not upload anything private, secret or personal. Because the files are public, they may also be cached, copied or shared by other people and services, and I can't take those copies back.

If you only view files

Browsing and downloading needs no account. I don't run ads, trackers or analytics, and I don't set cookies. Like any web server, the hosting and network providers below may see your IP address in their normal request logs.

If you upload with the website

For each upload I record your IP address, the file name, size and path, the upload method, your browser's user agent, and the time. I use this only to deal with abuse, for example to ban an address that uploads something harmful. I don't sell or share it.

To block VPNs and proxies, your IP address is sent to proxycheck.io, a third-party lookup service, when you upload.

If you upload with GitHub sync

I receive the OIDC token from your GitHub Actions run and read your GitHub username, account ID and repository names from it, plus the IP address of the request. I store these to approve, revoke or ban accounts. I don't get your password, your code, or anything else from your account.

What stays in your browser

The site saves a few things in your browser's local storage, not in cookies: your visit history, and a cached copy of the file list so pages open faster. This data stays on your device. You can clear it any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Who else handles the data

  • Hugging Face stores the uploaded files.
  • Render hosts the website and its servers.
  • proxycheck.io checks upload IPs for VPNs and proxies.
  • GitHub provides the identity for GitHub sync.

These services have their own privacy policies. I may also look at uploaded files when needed, for example to check a report or remove abuse.

How long I keep things

I don't have a fixed deletion schedule. Upload records are kept for as long as I need them to handle abuse, and a ban record is kept for as long as the ban lasts. Uploaded files stay until I remove them.

Removing a file or asking a question

If you want a file removed, or you want to ask about your data, open an issue at github.com/notamitgamer/cdnV2, message @notamitgamer on GitHub, or contact me on Discord. I'll remove files that are illegal, abusive or that you uploaded yourself and want gone. Include the file's link so I can find it. Copies that others already made are out of my hands.

Changes

I may update this page. The date at the top shows the latest version. See also the Terms of Use.
